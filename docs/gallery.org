#+title: Gallery

Every interactive part of the desktop, one picture each. All of them are taken by =desktop-shots= in a staged session with made-up data; see [[file:testing.org][testing]]. Pictures are AVIF; any current browser shows them.

* Interactions

Short clips of the things a still cannot show.

** Drag to place

Super and drag a tiled window: the hypr-strict-resize plugin draws an outline where it will land, and it lands there when you let go.

#+HTML: <img src="../screenshots/gallery/drag-to-place.avif" alt="Drag to place (animation)">

** Border resizing

Dragging the border between two tiled windows moves only that border.

#+HTML: <img src="../screenshots/gallery/resize.avif" alt="Border resizing (animation)">

** Pill buttons

Hover for the tooltip, click for the card (here the calendar), right-click for the quick action (here play/pause on the music pill).

#+HTML: <img src="../screenshots/gallery/pills.avif" alt="Pill buttons (animation)">

** Do Not Disturb

With Do Not Disturb on, a message arrives without a popup; the bell pill counts it.

#+HTML: <img src="../screenshots/gallery/dnd.avif" alt="Do Not Disturb (animation)">

* Bar popups

Every pill's click card (=bar popup NAME= opens one from a script). Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, battery, power profiles and music here are stand-ins: =desktop-shots-fakes= plays those services.
